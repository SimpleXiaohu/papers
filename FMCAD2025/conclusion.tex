This paper has strived to provide a concise introduction of the string solver 
OSTRICH2, which inputs constraints in an extension of the SMT-LIB theory of 
Unicode Strings with transducers (through mutual recursion) and ECMAScript
regular expressions, among others. We have also empirically demonstrated its 
competitiveness with other string solvers on SMT-COMP benchmarks, particularly 
on unsatisfiable instances. Interested readers, who are interested in
participating in the development and/or applications of OSTRICH2, are
wholeheartedly encouraged to get in touch with us.
